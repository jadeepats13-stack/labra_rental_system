How booking works

  1. Create an account

    Sign up with your name, username and email. It takes a minute.

  2. Pick a free time

    See which hours are open this week, with the weather forecast for each day.

  3. Pay

    Pay online with PayPal, or pay in cash at the court before you play.

  4. Show your QR code

    Your booking email has a QR code. Staff scan it and your playing time starts.

One court, painted for the game

The LA Court is an outdoor court surrounded by trees in Tambacan. Bookings are for the whole court, in 30-minute steps between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM.
